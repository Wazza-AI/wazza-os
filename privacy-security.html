    Privacy and security

    Wazza OS protects your computer by giving agents a computer of their own: the VM. Agents work there freely; what reaches your real computer (your files, your screen) goes through you.

    What runs where#

    WhereWhat runs thereWhat it can reach
    The VMThe apps (GIMP, Chromium…), the Hermes agent, every agent's commands and file workThe VM's own files and the Internet. Not your computer's files, not your screen.
    Your computer, inside Wazza OS's sandboxThe agent programs of some Chats (for example Claude Code and Codex), and the Computer Control ChatTheir own folder (bubblewrap and Landlock), the Internet and Wazza OS. Not your other files; not services of your computer unless you turn on Local services.
    Your computerThe Wazza OS window, the VM's engine, Wazza OS's own servicesIts own folders; it never changes your files (what it writes is always new files).
    The AI provider you choseThe modelWhat the agent sends it: your messages, and the content the agent reads or looks at to answer (files, pictures of an app). Its own policy applies (your plan's or key's).

    Agents in the VM work without asking#

    A Chat's agent runs its commands and edits files in the VM without asking you each time: Wazza OS has no "ask mode". The VM is the protection: what an agent does there cannot touch your own computer's files. For mistakes inside the VM, Wazza OS keeps the versions of files that get overwritten and a checkpoint of every file a Chat edits (Versions and undo), and you can back up a whole VM (Backups).

    Where Wazza OS asks you#

    Inside the VM, agents never ask: they run commands, install and uninstall apps, write to other tabs' Chats and take steps that cannot be undone (deleting, sending) without a card. When you want to approve something first, say so in your message ("show me the plan before you install anything"). What reaches your own computer is different:

    WhenWhat happensWho decides
    Files from your computer go into the VM (your paste, an agent's copy)By your rule in Files & sharing: Allow (default), Ask, or Off. Attaching a file to a Chat is your own pick in a file chooser.You
    Files from the VM go to your computer (your paste, an agent's upload)A question each time by default (Ask); they land in Downloads/From the VM, never over your filesYou
    An agent wants to see or use your screenThe Computer Control consent card, per tab, and again after every restartOnly your own click on Start
    A sandboxed Chat on your computer wants to edit your filesLet this Chat edit my files in its model window (with a confirmation); off again at every Wazza OS startOnly you
    Local services, importing your Chrome profileSettings switches and buttons that only your own click turns on (an agent can only turn Local services off)Only you

    Keys and sign-ins#

    • API keys, plan sign-ins, custom API tokens and your Wazza OS account are kept on your computer, in Wazza OS's encrypted store.
    • Agents in the VM never get the key itself: their requests go through Wazza OS, which adds it.
    • The Sign in page shows a key by its last characters only; the command line takes keys on standard input only, never as a word of a command.
    • Company folder logins stay on this computer, encrypted; web app sign-ins live in the VM's browser (Sign out of all web apps ends them).

    What leaves your computer#

    WhatTo whereWhen
    Your messages and what agents read to answerThe AI provider of the Chat's modelWhen you use a Chat
    Your skills, files and Chat historyYour Wazza OS account's vaultOnly when you connected sync (Chat history can be turned off)
    FeedbackWazza, without your account (only Wazza OS's version is added)When you send feedback
    Update checksWazza OS's download serverAt start and every hour (updates are signed and checked before install)
    Team activity totals (active and idle time, input counts; never content)Your team's vaultOnly on a team account whose policy turns it on

    The app journal (Wazza OS noting the menus, clicks and keys you use in apps, never a password) stays on your computer to teach agents; pause it or delete it in Settings › Skills & learning › App journal. To erase everything: Remove all data.
